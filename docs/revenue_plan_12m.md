## Math

**Quotes needed per month**  
= Monthly target ÷ Close rate ÷ Average ticket  

Example (from spec):  
- Target $180,000/year → $15,000/month  
- Average job $750  
- Close rate 40%  
- Quotes/month ≈ 15,000 / 0.4 / 750 = **50**  
- Jobs/month = 50 × 0.4 = **20**
